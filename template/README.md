# My Plugin

Inserts a greeting at the cursor and counts the words in the open note.

## What it can reach

- **commands**: adds two commands to the command palette and the slash menu.
- **editor**: reads the open note and inserts text at the cursor.
- **ui**: shows a notification and asks who the greeting is for.

It sends nothing over the network and stores nothing.

## How to use it

1. Turn it on in **Settings → Plugins** and approve what it asks for.
2. Open a note, press `Cmd+P` (`Ctrl+P` on Windows and Linux), and run **Insert a greeting** or **Count words in this note**.
